Privacy Policy

Case Manager Tool | Last Updated: November 9, 2026

RHENZ Digital Solution respects the privacy of individuals and is committed to explaining how information may be collected, stored, processed, and protected when using the Case Manager Tool.

1. Information We Handle

The Case Manager Tool may handle information entered or imported by its authorized users, including household profiles, family composition, case management information, and generated Social Case Study Reports (SCSR).

Users are responsible for ensuring that they have proper authority to access and process the information they enter into the application.

2. Local Storage of Household Records

Household records and generated SCSR documents are intended to be stored locally on the Municipal Link's computer.

These records are not automatically uploaded to RHENZ Digital Solution's website or subscription database as part of the intended local-storage workflow.

Users should protect their computers, maintain appropriate backups, and prevent unauthorized access to confidential household information.

3. Artificial Intelligence Services

The Case Manager Tool is designed to support three AI processing options. The AI service selected and configured by the user determines how information submitted for AI processing is handled.

Groq API

If a user selects Groq, information submitted for AI processing may be transmitted to Groq through its API. Groq's own privacy policy and service terms govern its processing of submitted information.

Google Gemini API

If a user selects Google Gemini, information submitted for AI processing may be transmitted to Google through its API. Google's applicable privacy policy and API terms govern its processing.

Local Ollama

If a user selects Local Ollama, AI processing is intended to take place on the user's computer using a locally installed AI model. This option does not require sending prompts to Groq or Gemini.

Users should review AI-generated content before using or finalizing any case management document.

4. Personal API Keys

Users who choose Groq or Google Gemini are expected to obtain and provide their own API keys through the application's AI Settings.

API access, usage limits, and related charges are governed by the respective AI provider. Such charges are separate from the purchase price or subscription fee of the Case Manager Tool.

Users should keep their API keys confidential and should not share them with unauthorized persons.

5. Subscription and Device Information

The Case Manager Tool may communicate with Supabase services to support device registration, trial management, license validation, and subscription administration.

Information involved in these operations may include a device fingerprint or identifier, device name, operating platform, application version, subscription status, and trial-generation count.

This information is separate from the locally stored household records and SCSR documents.

6. Third-Party Services

The application may rely on third-party services, including:

  • Supabase for subscription and device-related services.
  • Groq for optional AI processing.
  • Google Gemini for optional AI processing.
  • Ollama for local AI processing.

Each third-party provider has its own terms, privacy practices, and security policies. Users are encouraged to review them before using their services.

7. Data Security and User Responsibility

RHENZ Digital Solution aims to incorporate appropriate safeguards into the Case Manager Tool. However, no software or electronic storage system can guarantee absolute security.

Users should secure their computers, protect their login credentials and API keys, and follow applicable organizational policies on handling confidential and personal information.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored household records and generated documents remain subject to the user's computer storage and data-management practices.

Subscription and device-related records may be retained in the subscription system as necessary for license administration and related operations.

9. Updates to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as the Case Manager Tool develops or as its features and data-handling practices change. The latest version will be published on the official RHENZ Digital Solution website.

10. Contact

For questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy or the Case Manager Tool, please contact RHENZ Digital Solution through its official website or GitHub profile.
